Important Information
About Our Handmade Products

Every Kordiva creation is made with patience, care and human hands. That means no two pieces are ever perfectly identical — and that's exactly what makes them special.

01
HANDMADE UNIQUENESS

Every Piece Is Uniquely Handmade

Each Kordiva product is carefully handmade. Therefore, even if you order five pieces of the same design, they may not be exactly identical. We do not promise machine-like uniformity, as every piece is crafted by hand.

02
PRODUCT APPEARANCE

Slight Differences From Product Photos

The product you receive will be approximately 98–99% similar to the product shown in the photos. Due to the handmade process, there may be very slight differences in shape, knots, weaving, texture, or finishing.

03
COLOUR & LIGHT

Colour May Vary Slightly

The actual colour of a product may appear slightly different from the photos due to lighting conditions, photography, screen brightness, and individual display settings. We always try to show the product as accurately as possible.

04
NATURAL CHARACTER

Natural Handmade Imperfections

Small variations, uneven knots, minor differences in texture, or slight irregularities may occur. These are not defects but natural characteristics of handmade craftsmanship.

05
PRODUCT DIMENSIONS

Measurements May Vary Slightly

Although we provide product dimensions, a small variation in measurements may occur because every piece is handmade. We always try to keep the product as close as possible to the listed dimensions.
