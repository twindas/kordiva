Returns & Refund Policy

Your satisfaction matters to us.

Return Window

If you receive a damaged, defective or incorrect product, please contact Kordiva as soon as possible after delivery.

Eligibility

Products must be returned in their original condition and should be unused, where applicable. Handmade products may have natural variations that are not considered defects.

Damaged Products

If your product arrives damaged, please contact us with photographs and your order details so that we can review the issue.

Refunds

Once a return or refund request has been reviewed and approved, the applicable refund process will be initiated.

Non-Returnable Items

Certain personalised, customised or specially prepared products may not be eligible for return.

Contact Us

For return or refund assistance, please contact Kordiva with your order number and details of the issue.
